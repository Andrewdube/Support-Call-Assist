SAFETY FIRST: advise the customer to power off, unplug, stop using and not charge the device, and keep it away from heat and flammable materials. Do not puncture or press on the battery.
1. Confirm visible swelling (touchpad or keyboard lifting, gap in chassis).
2. Swollen batteries are typically replaced regardless of warranty status per safety policy - follow your entitlement/safety process.
3. Arrange onsite technician or depot service with hazardous-shipping approved packaging; customer should not ship it themselves in standard packaging.
4. Order battery; add C-cover/keyboard bezel if chassis is deformed.
